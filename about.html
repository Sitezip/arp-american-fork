About Us

We are service missionaries and facilitators of the American Fork Mission of the Addiction Recovery Program, helping individuals and families find hope and healing through Jesus Christ.

Our purpose

This local page exists so that participants, friends, and family can find all of our American Fork area meetings in one place, learn what to expect, and feel welcome to just show up.

Part of the official Addiction Recovery Program

Our meetings are part of Healing through the Savior: The Addiction Recovery Program of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and use its official guidebooks. Official information, stories, and a worldwide meeting finder are at addictionrecovery.churchofjesuschrist.org. This website is a local resource and is not an official Church website.

Our mission

  • 50 service missionaries—individuals and couples, all over 18 years old—serve in the American Fork Mission.
  • We hold 20 weekly meetings throughout American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Cedar Hills, Highland and Lehi, both in person and on Zoom.
  • Each meeting is supported by two missionaries and one facilitator.

Interested in serving?

Requirements to serve:

  • A completed application
  • An interview with the Bishop and Stake President
  • A minimum commitment of 3 hours per week out of the home

Qualifications: No prior knowledge of addiction is required—just a desire to serve and love the participants.
