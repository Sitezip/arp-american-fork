Welcome! The American Fork Mission Addiction Recovery Program holds 20 free weekly meetings in American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Cedar Hills, Highland, and Lehi, plus meetings on Zoom and by phone.

Just show up

There is no cost, and you do not need to be referred or sign up to attend. Come to any meeting, any week. We use first names only, and what is shared in the meeting stays in the meeting. You can simply listen—it's okay to say "pass."

Meetings follow the 12 steps in Healing through the Savior: The Addiction Recovery Program, centered on Jesus Christ. There are meetings for people seeking recovery and support meetings for spouses, parents, and family members.

Friends and family: this page has all of our local meetings in one place—feel free to share it.
